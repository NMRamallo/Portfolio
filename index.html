About Me

I build AI systems that do real work inside a company — assistants people actually use, wired into the tools they already have.

At Cintelink I design and operate LLM-powered automations: an internal Slack assistant, now in its second iteration; retrieval over company documentation so answers are grounded in real sources instead of model memory; and integrations that let the model act on Jira, Drive and internal services rather than just reply. Most of it is orchestrated in n8n.

Before AI I spent about two years in Unreal Engine and C++, building replicated multiplayer systems. That background is why I treat LLM work as engineering: state, latency and failure modes are familiar problems wearing new clothes.

I try to measure before adopting. When I evaluated GraphRAG as an upgrade to our retrieval pipeline, the gains didn't justify the added complexity and cost — so I recommended against it. Knowing when not to add a technology is part of the job.
